Warbell v0.24.0 gives the opening three days concrete goals and consequences, rebuilds the third-person knight, and fixes destroyed buildings and world collisions.

## New — the opening campaign

- Rescue villagers from a nearby camp, bring them home and put a farm to work. Progress follows actual rescues, returning workers and food production.
- Prepare for torch bearers targeting your fields on the second night. Buy a permanent War Table upgrade and see the outcome reported at dawn.
- On day three, raid the marked shaman camp to remove its shamans from that night's assault, or purchase ranged support and defend against the full attack.
- The first three preparation days stay in clear daylight without a countdown. Ring the war bell with E whenever you are ready, even with an unfinished suggested task. Later preparation returns to its normal clock.
- A short objective tracker and named compass targets explain the current situation. J opens optional details; the detailed opening tracker retires after the third night.
- Thirteen recorded hero lines add briefings, reactions and occasional reminders. Advice updates with your progress and stops when combat starts; obsolete instructions and overlapping dialogue are cancelled.

## Hero and night threats

- The third-person hero is now a textured Royal Footman with articulated armour, rebuilt boots and shoulder plates, and revised walking, running and shield-block poses.
- The sword stays in its carry position during walking and running.
- Opening assaults progress from scouts to farm raiders, shamans and a two-front attack. Later nights rotate specialist formations.
- Daytime raids suppress the announced specialists for the promised night. Ordinary attackers remain, and reduced waves still finish normally.
- First-tier Keep Archers now cost 80 gold, making ranged support attainable from normal early-night income.

## Fixed — buildings, scenery and saves

- Destroyed producers leave visible, walkable ruins. Their old collision shell disappears immediately, and nearby ruins offer rebuilding during preparation when resources allow.
- Rebuilding restores the correct building and collision. Loading saves restores structures and ruins, including after a map change; same-frame damage no longer leaves orphan flames.
- Solid scenery uses collision footprints measured from its mesh and rotation. Placement reserves room for visible props and story locations, reducing overlapping objects.
- Ork torches have a shaped wooden shaft, wrapped grip and metal head, with tapered, flickering flame tongues.
- Campaign progress and staging-camp raid outcomes survive save/load. Older saves keep their normal preparation flow without restarting the introduction.
- Loading a run drops abandoned dialogue instead of replaying old briefings or rewards.

The Windows installer is self-signed. Windows SmartScreen may show **Unknown Publisher**; choose **More info → Run anyway** to install.
