Third Warbell 1.0 preview build. It brings a new knight run, orks that walk and charge with Mixamo gaits, a steadier first-person view and less work per frame.

## Knight

- Running now uses Mixamo's own "Running" animation on the knight's rig, played at its natural pace. The separate jog stage is gone, and the boots stay planted from walk through sprint.
- Sprinting covers ground with longer bounds at the same speed.
- First person: the eye now sits at the visor, the shield steps aside when guarding, and no sword or shield pieces cut through the view.

## Orks

- Orks walk and charge with new Mixamo "Orc Walk" and "Mutant Run" animations.

## Performance

- Less work per frame: materials and transforms are no longer rewritten every frame, so the game does less on the CPU.

## Under the hood

- Ship-cabin cutscene film (P.0 "Below Deck") with talking faces and lip-sync. It is used for the trailer film and is not triggered in game.
- Filmed cutscenes now step the simulation by exactly one frame, and the clip recorder captures the game's own audio in step with the video.

The Windows installer is self-signed. Windows SmartScreen may show **Unknown Publisher**; choose **More info → Run anyway** to install.
