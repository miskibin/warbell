Warbell v0.23.1 fixes the title-screen composition, distant haze and duplicate first-person sword.

## Title screen

- A closer in-engine view of the keep and war bell replaces the distant, sun-dominated backdrop. The left side leaves room for the title and actions.
- The backdrop keeps its proportions on wider and narrower screens. The menu column scales to fit smaller windows.
- Gameplay health bars, resource counters and compass stay hidden while the title is open, then return with their previous visibility when play resumes.

## World and first-person fixes

- Distant haze retains terrain and object colours and shading. The two fog layers and sun in-scatter no longer combine into opaque pale silhouettes; atmospheric depth and biome/time-of-day moods remain.
- First person consistently hides the animated third-person weapon, including during running, camera transitions and equipment rebuilds. The camera's own sword stays visible, and third-person gestures retain their normal weapon policy.

The Windows installer is self-signed. Windows SmartScreen may show **Unknown Publisher**; choose **More info → Run anyway** to install.
