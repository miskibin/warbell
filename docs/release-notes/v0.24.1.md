Warbell v0.24.1 restores the island’s vegetation and fixes the Royal Footman’s walking and running gait.

## World

- Trees once again grow among bushes and small ground decorations. The previous collision update accidentally treated their visible footprints as tree exclusion zones, thinning the woods and undergrowth.
- Walkable vegetation can layer naturally. Buildings, large rocks, roads and story clearings retain their placement checks and solid collisions.
- The fix applies when the world is generated, including when continuing a saved campaign; no new game is required.

## Hero

- Walking and running use the imported footman’s actual leg proportions. Supporting boots stay near the ground, the recovery leg lifts, and the ankles counter-rotate to keep the soles level.
- Idle, walking and running blend smoothly. Other troops retain their existing locomotion.

The Windows installer is self-signed. Windows SmartScreen may show **Unknown Publisher**; choose **More info → Run anyway** to install.
